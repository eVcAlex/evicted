Bottom of the Week

Rose faces the £2 levy as Vardy Party fold to a net 31

Bench points unspent, a four-point hit taken in vain: the week's lowest return lands squarely with the champagne set.

Danny Rose's Vardy Party posted the lowest net score of gameweek six, a return of 31 points that leaves the manager liable for the customary £2 fine into the season pot. A gross of 35 was undone by a four-point transfer hit and twelve points left idle on the bench.

It is the second consecutive week the side has propped up the table. Midfield General, on 36, escaped the drop by the narrowest of margins. The pot now stands at £84.00, with seven of twelve managers having settled their season buy-in.
